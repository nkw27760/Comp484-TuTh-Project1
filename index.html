Welcome, Pilot.

QUICK START LANCER is a tabletop roleplaying game created by Miguel Lopez and Tom Bloom about mech pilots, political exploitation, and the difficult choices people need to make in a world where corpo-states and renegades alike vie for control over the outskirts of a supposed utopia. I made this guide as crash-course way to learn the "shape" of the game before you get into the finer details of fleshing out your character, refining your build, and jumping into your first session of actual gameplay.

You do not need to memorize every single rule, and you don't need to play your character perfectly. Start with the basics, it's impossible to be a paragon player right off the bat. Give yourself permission to not know everything. Focus on just having fun, the rest will come naturally with time.

Four steps before takeoff:

  1. Learn about the setting, the powers that be, and how your character will fit within it all.
  2. Learn about the interplay between the pilot side and mech side of the game, and how each contributes to the campaign narrative.
  3. Learn how and what to prep to bring to your campaign's first session.
  4. Learn what you should expect at the table and get ready for mission deployment.
